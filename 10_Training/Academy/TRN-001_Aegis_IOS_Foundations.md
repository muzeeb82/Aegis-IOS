## 1. Audience and Outcomes

For product owners, architects, engineers, operators, reviewers, and authorized investment-process users. Learners will distinguish authority from execution, navigate the repository, create a traceable decision, identify AI/connector boundaries, validate evidence, and respond to failure without overclaiming completion.

## 2. Modules

1. Constitution, governance, authority, and canonical ownership.
2. Product goals, capabilities, FR/AC/NFR traceability.
3. Modular architecture and systems of record.
4. Decision, evidence, portfolio, framework, and learning records.
5. AI independence, prompt injection, and promotion boundaries.
6. Connectors, provenance, idempotency, and reconciliation.
7. Security, authorization, audit, and privacy.
8. Validation evidence, defects, recovery, and release gates.

## 3. Exercises

- Trace FR-009 from PRD through specifications to a test case.
- Diagnose an invalid decision transition.
- Identify provenance missing from synthetic evidence.
- Review an AI output attempting to exceed tool authority.
- Reconcile an ambiguous connector timeout.
- Execute EX-001 and produce a validation record.

## 4. Assessment

Completion requires at least 80% on knowledge questions and a Passed practical exercise with no critical error in authority, provenance, lifecycle, security, or result truthfulness. The threshold is a training criterion, not a product performance target.
