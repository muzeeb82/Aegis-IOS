# CONST-001 — Aegis IOS Constitution

**Owner:** Architecture Governance  
**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Purpose

Establish the enduring principles that govern Aegis IOS.

## Principles

- Architecture before implementation.
- One rule has one owner and one authoritative artifact.
- Evidence precedes opinion.
- Documentation, validation, and training are product requirements.
- Knowledge is durable; implementations are replaceable.

## Change control

This constitution changes only through an approved RFC and ADR.
