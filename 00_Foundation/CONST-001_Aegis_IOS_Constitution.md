## 1. Document Purpose

This Constitution establishes the enduring purpose, vision, mission, core values, and long-term philosophy of Aegis IOS.

## 2. Aegis IOS Purpose

Aegis IOS exists to provide an enterprise-grade, AI-agnostic Investment Operating System that governs investment decision workflows through disciplined architecture, evidence, traceability, and continuous improvement.

## 3. Vision

To become an AI-independent Investment Operating System capable of supporting multiple investment strategies, portfolios, brokers, AI models, and future users through disciplined architecture, governance, documentation, and continuous learning.

## 4. Mission

Design, validate, document, and evolve a scalable, maintainable, explainable, auditable, and extensible Investment Operating System.

## 5. Core Values

- Architecture before implementation.
- Single source of truth.
- One rule has one owner.
- Knowledge is permanent; implementations are replaceable.
- Evidence before opinion.
- Documentation is part of the product.
- Every feature must be teachable.
- Every release must be validated.
- Long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.

## 6. Long-Term Philosophy

Aegis IOS is a documentation-first software product. Its governed knowledge is durable; its technology, models, providers, and implementations may evolve without becoming the source of truth.

## 7. Governance Boundary

This Constitution owns foundational philosophy. Operational governance, authority rules, concept ownership, and repository evolution are governed by GOV-001 and its approved successors.

## 8. Change Control

This Constitution may change only through the approved governance change process and an Architecture Decision Record.
